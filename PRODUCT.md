## Product

Pumiput Chaichat's bilingual personal website. It presents him first through
places, light, and moments he chooses to remember, then later expands into
About and Work experiences.

## Current Scope

Home is the active content experience during this phase. About Me, Portfolio,
and Work are linked from the primary navigation as dedicated blank white pages
reserved for their future content.

## Audience

Recruiters, collaborators, friends, and visitors who want to understand the
person behind the developer. They may arrive on desktop or mobile and should
understand the mood and identity within the first viewport.

## Direction

The site is a Cinematic Personal Journal Portfolio:

- Photography is the page structure, not decoration.
- Home feels like a bright travel journal with full-viewport image chapters.
- Copy is personal, short, and placed only where the image has negative space.
- The interface is minimal, calm, and precise.
- Dark sections use neutral near-black, never dark green.

## Success

A visitor remembers the atmosphere, understands that the site belongs to
Pumiput, explores the journal chapters, and reaches Facebook or Instagram.

## Constraints

- Pure PHP, HTML, CSS, and Vanilla JavaScript.
- Thai and English are first-class.
- Facebook and Instagram are the only contact channels.
- Respect reduced motion and keyboard navigation.
- No generic portfolio cards, skill bars, tech-logo walls, or project lists on
  Home.
